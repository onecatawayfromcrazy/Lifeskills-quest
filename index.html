✨ How to earn stars

Memorise a lesson answer (+5 ⭐), solve its clickable word mission (+3 ⭐), master a word-bank term (+2 ⭐), flip and learn a flashcard (+1 ⭐), or solve a quiz question for the first time (+1 ⭐). Stars are saved in this browser — no sign-in or internet needed.
